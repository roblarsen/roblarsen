# Hi, I'm Rob Larsen 👋

I am a long-time open-source maintainer, published technical author, and enterprise technology leader based in Boston. For over twenty years, my work has centered on the craftsmanship of the web: establishing early front-end engineering standards, maintaining foundational open-source tooling, and architecting resilient enterprise platforms.

---

## 🛠️ Open-Source Stewardship & Core Projects

- **HTML5 Boilerplate ([@h5bp](https://github.com/h5bp)):** Owner of the organization and primary maintainer across core repositories, including `html5-boilerplate`, `create-html5-boilerplate`, and `main.css`. (150k+ stars across the org and still shipping.)
- **[Front-end Developer Interview Questions](https://github.com/h5bp/Front-end-Developer-Interview-Questions):** Longtime maintainer of the industry's default open-source resource for evaluating engineering talent across the stack.
- **[Assumptions & Constraints Manifest (ACM)](https://github.com/roblarsen):** Author and maintainer of the ACM specification and CLI tooling, designed to bring type-safe, auditable governance to AI-assisted code generation.
- **Specialized Data Pipelines & Numeric Architecture:** Building open TypeScript infrastructure, large-number/Googology tools, and schema architectures that model asset provenance, census tracking, and market velocity.
